Section 01

Who We Are

Lexoral is an independent legal transcription provider operating across France, Montréal (Québec, Canada), and the United States. We provide certified French–English bilingual transcription services for legal proceedings including depositions, arbitrations, tribunal hearings, and cross-border litigation.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexoral collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information in the course of providing our services. It applies to all clients, deponents, legal professionals, and individuals whose personal information is processed by Lexoral.

Note: This policy is governed by Québec's Law 25 on personal information protection (Loi modernisant des dispositions législatives en matière de protection des renseignements personnels), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable, and Canada's PIPEDA.

Section 02

Information We Collect

Lexoral collects only the information strictly necessary to provide transcription services and manage our client relationships. We collect information in the following categories:

Client and Contact Information

  • Name, title, and law firm or organization
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Billing address and payment information (processed securely; card data never stored by Lexoral)
  • Matter reference numbers and file identifiers

Proceeding Materials

  • Audio and video recordings of legal proceedings submitted for transcription
  • Names, titles, and spoken content of all participants in submitted recordings
  • Exhibits, reference documents, and terminology glossaries provided by clients
  • Completed transcripts and associated certification documents

Technical Information

  • IP address and browser type (collected when using our web intake form)
  • File metadata associated with uploaded recordings
  • Communication records (emails, WhatsApp messages) related to a matter
Section 03

How We Use Information

All personal information collected by Lexoral is used exclusively for purposes directly related to the delivery of transcription services. Specifically, we use information to:

  • Produce accurate, certified bilingual transcripts of submitted proceedings
  • Communicate with clients about their matter, turnaround, and delivery
  • Issue invoices and process payments
  • Maintain chain-of-custody documentation for evidentiary purposes
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
  • Improve our internal quality assurance processes (using anonymized, non-identifiable data only)

We never use client recordings, transcripts, or personal information for artificial intelligence training, product development, advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside the specific transcription engagement.

Section 04

Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR applies (including for clients and individuals in the European Union or France), Lexoral processes personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to deliver the transcription services requested
  • Legitimate interests — maintaining business records, chain-of-custody documentation, and quality assurance
  • Legal obligation — compliance with applicable law in Canada, France, and the United States
  • Consent — where required for specific processing activities, obtained explicitly and recorded

Under Québec's Law 25, Lexoral acts as the person responsible for the protection of personal information and has designated internal procedures to ensure compliance.

Section 05

Confidentiality of Proceedings

Lexoral treats all submitted recordings and transcripts as strictly confidential. Given the sensitive nature of legal proceedings, we apply heightened protections to proceeding materials:

  • Access to proceeding materials is restricted to the specific personnel assigned to a matter
  • No recording or transcript is shared with any party other than the instructing client
  • Personnel handling materials are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Clients may request permanent deletion of all materials at the conclusion of an engagement
  • Materials are never used in marketing, testimonials, or case studies without explicit written consent

Where a proceeding is subject to a court order, protective order, or confidentiality agreement, clients should notify us at intake. We will comply with all applicable restrictions and can accommodate sealed or in camera matter designations.

Section 06

Data Sharing

Lexoral does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — encrypted file transfer and storage providers operating under strict confidentiality agreements and data processing agreements compliant with applicable law
  • Payment processors — PCI-compliant processors for billing; no payment card data is stored by Lexoral
  • Legal requirements — where disclosure is required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, in which case we will notify the client to the extent permitted by law

All third-party service providers are contractually bound to use personal information only for the purpose of providing services to Lexoral and to apply equivalent data protection standards.

Section 07

International Transfers

Lexoral operates across France, Québec (Canada), and the United States. Personal information may be transferred between these jurisdictions in the course of service delivery. We ensure such transfers are protected by:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU-to-non-EU transfers
  • Compliance with the requirements of Québec's Law 25 for cross-border transfers of personal information
  • End-to-end encryption for all file transfers regardless of jurisdiction
Section 08

Data Retention

Lexoral retains personal information and proceeding materials only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which they were collected. Our full retention schedule is set out in our Data Retention Policy. In summary:

  • Completed transcripts and certification documents are retained for a default period of 2 years
  • Audio recordings submitted for transcription are deleted within 30 days of transcript delivery unless otherwise requested
  • Client contact and billing information is retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes
  • Clients may request permanent deletion of proceeding materials at any time
Section 09

Security

Lexoral implements technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of legal proceeding materials. These include:

  • End-to-end encryption for all file transfers and communications
  • Access controls limiting proceeding material access to assigned personnel only
  • Secure, access-restricted storage environments with audit logging
  • Regular review of security procedures and personnel training
  • Incident response procedures in compliance with Law 25 and GDPR notification requirements

In the event of a personal information incident that presents a risk of serious harm, Lexoral will notify affected individuals and the applicable supervisory authority within the timeframes required by law.

Section 10

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal information held by Lexoral:

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right of rectification — to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right of deletion — to request permanent deletion of your personal information and proceeding materials
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your information in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — to receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format (GDPR)
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at secure@lexoral.org. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information (Québec), the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (France/CNIL), or the applicable supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

Section 11

Cookies

Our website uses only essential technical cookies necessary for its operation. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that collect personal information. No cookie consent banner is required as no non-essential cookies are deployed.

Section 12

Changes to This Policy

Lexoral may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Material changes will be communicated to active clients by email. The effective date at the top of this document will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our services following notification of a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
