Certified by an official stenographer

Certified transcripts

[LX.9]

For Quebec court filings. A transcript to be filed before a Quebec court is certified by an official stenographer accredited by the Barreau du Québec. Lexoral handles the request from start to finish, with official stenographers accredited in French and English.

L.01

When certification is required

Quebec reserves the official record to accredited stenographers. The Barreau’s Comité sur la sténographie grants the accreditation, and grants it language by language.

B-1, r. 13

“The accreditation is valid for the methods and languages indicated thereon.”

Regulation respecting the training, skill and knowledge evaluation, accreditation and discipline of stenographers, CQLR c. B-1, r. 13, s. 1 · enabling Act: Stenographers’ Act, CQLR c. S-33, s. 3

Whether a filing calls for a stenographer’s certificate depends on the forum and on what the transcript is for: an appeal record, an application for judicial review, a transcript filed in evidence. A transcript for the Court of Appeal, the Superior Court, the Court of Québec or a municipal court goes to an official stenographer from the start. The court’s registry or your counsel can confirm whether an attested transcript is accepted or a certified transcript is required.

L.02

How it works

  1. Request. Send the recording, the court and the deadline. We confirm the same business day.
  2. Preparation. Lexoral prepares the bilingual transcript in the court’s format, in strict confidence.
  3. Certification. An official stenographer accredited in French and English checks it against the recording and certifies it. We handle the rest.

When you need a transcript to work from rather than one to file, we prepare it ourselves, with our signed declaration of accuracy.

L.03

For an appeal

In civil matters, the appellant has 45 days after the judgment to certify that an official stenographer has been instructed to transcribe the depositions the appellant intends to use, or that no transcript is necessary. Where leave to appeal is required, the certificate is filed within 15 days of the judgment granting leave.

Art. 353 C.C.P.

“… within 45 days after the date of the judgment under appeal …”

Court of Appeal of Quebec, aide-memoire in civil matters · Code of Civil Procedure, CQLR c. C-25.01, arts. 353 and 357 · Regulation of the Court of Appeal of Quebec in Civil Matters, ss. 35 and 54 · courdappelduquebec.ca

In the brief, stenographic notes are reproduced page by page; the “four-in-one” layout is not permitted. We prepare the transcript of the depositions in the Court’s format, an official stenographer certifies it, and we confirm the delivery date in writing before we begin, so that the appeal schedule holds.

L.04

Both languages, one certificate

Because accreditation is granted by language, a stenographer accredited in French alone is not accredited for the English passages of a bilingual hearing, and only a minority of the roll holds both. The stenographers Lexoral works with hold accreditation in both languages: a bilingual record is certified by one person, in one certificate.

L.05

Where the recordings come from

Hearings recorded by a tribunal and obtained from its registry, and examinations recorded by the parties. Send the file as you received it; we work from the original audio.

Where the hearing took place makes no difference: we take files from every judicial district, from Gatineau to Gaspé and from Sherbrooke to Saguenay, Montreal, Quebec City and Trois-Rivières included.

L.06

Questions

Is Lexoral an official stenographer?
No. Lexoral is a legal transcription firm. The transcript is certified by an official stenographer on the Barreau du Québec’s roll of stenographers; we prepare it and handle everything, from the request to delivery.
Can a transcript made from a recording be certified?
Yes. Official stenographers transcribe recordings as well as hearings they attend. The certificate says the transcript was prepared from a recording, so the court knows exactly what it is reading.
Do I need a certified transcript?
That depends on the forum and on what the transcript is for. The court’s registry or your counsel can confirm whether an attested transcript is accepted or a certified transcript is required. We do not give legal advice.
How long does it take?
We confirm the date in writing once the audio has been heard, before work begins: usually five business days, and 48 hours where the recording allows.
What does it cost?
The quote for a certified transcript follows a review of the audio; the certification is included.
Do you prepare transcripts for an appeal?
Yes. In civil matters, the appellant must certify within 45 days of the judgment that an official stenographer has been instructed to transcribe the depositions (art. 353 C.C.P.). Send us the recordings and the deadline: we confirm the delivery date in writing before we begin.
Do you work anywhere in Quebec?
Yes. We work from the recording, whatever the district: Montreal, Quebec City, Gatineau, Sherbrooke, Trois-Rivières, Saguenay or elsewhere.
