[Certified transcripts]

Certified by an official stenographer

For Quebec court filings. A transcript to be filed before a Quebec court is prepared and certified by an official stenographer accredited by the Barreau du Québec, who holds the mandate. Lexoral works in collaboration with an official stenographer accredited in French and English, and prepares the bilingual transcript.

L.01

When you need one

Quebec reserves the official record to accredited stenographers. The Barreau’s Comité sur la sténographie grants the accreditation, and grants it language by language.

B-1, r. 13

“The accreditation is valid for the methods and languages indicated thereon.”

Regulation respecting the training, skill and knowledge evaluation, accreditation and discipline of stenographers, CQLR c. B-1, r. 13, s. 1 · enabling Act: Stenographers’ Act, CQLR c. S-33, s. 3

Whether a filing calls for a stenographer’s certificate depends on the forum and on what the transcript is for: an appeal record, an application for judicial review, a transcript filed in evidence. A transcript for the Court of Appeal, the Superior Court, the Court of Québec or a municipal court goes to an official stenographer from the start. The court’s registry or your counsel can confirm whether an attested transcript is accepted or a certified transcript is required.

L.02

How it works

  1. Request. For a Quebec court filing, Lexoral transmits the request, with your consent, to its collaborating official stenographer, accredited in French and English.
  2. Mandate. They confirm the price and the date, bill you at the official tariff and answer for the transcript.
  3. Preparation. Lexoral prepares the bilingual transcript as the stenographer’s collaborator, under their mandate and a confidentiality agreement. They check it against the recording and certify it in their own name.

The mandate, the certificate and the responsibility for them are the stenographer’s own. When you need a transcript to work from rather than one to file, we prepare it ourselves, with our signed declaration of accuracy.

L.03

Both languages, one certificate

Because accreditation is granted by language, a stenographer accredited in French alone is not accredited for the English passages of a bilingual hearing, and only a minority of the roll holds both. The collaborating stenographer holds accreditation in both languages: a bilingual record is certified by one person, in one certificate.

L.05

Questions

Can a transcript made from a recording be certified?
Yes. Official stenographers transcribe recordings as well as hearings they attend. The certificate says the transcript was prepared from a recording, so the court knows exactly what it is reading.
Do I need a certified transcript?
That depends on the forum and on what the transcript is for. The court’s registry or your counsel can confirm whether an attested transcript is accepted or a certified transcript is required. We do not give legal advice.
How long does it take?
The stenographer sets the date on accepting the mandate, before any work begins.
What does it cost?
The stenographer quotes and bills the certified transcript at the official tariff. A working transcript ordered from us is quoted separately, once the audio has been reviewed.
