Privacy Policy

Compliance

[LX.1]

Effective September 27, 2026. Updated November 20, 2026.

L.01

Who we are

Lexoral is an independent legal transcription firm serving clients in Canada, including Quebec, and in the United States. We produce attested bilingual French–English transcripts of depositions, examinations, arbitrations, hearings and cross-border proceedings.

This policy explains what we hold, why we hold it and what you can ask of us. It is written to meet Quebec’s Law 25, Canada’s PIPEDA and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Brian Fino, co-founder — brian.fno@proton.me.

L.02

What we hold, and what we do with it

We hold your contact and billing details, the recordings, exhibits and glossaries you send us, the transcripts and attestations we produce, the words of everyone heard on a recording, and our correspondence about the file. We ask for nothing the file does not require, and we never hold card numbers.

We use it to produce and deliver your transcript, to keep chain-of-custody records, to invoice you and to meet our legal obligations. Nothing you send is used for any purpose beyond your file — not for development, not for training, not for marketing — and it never appears in a testimonial or a case study without your written consent.

Where the GDPR applies, we process this information to perform our contract with you, to meet our legal obligations and, for record-keeping and for making our services known to legal professionals, on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, we ask for it.

L.03

Confidentiality and disclosure

Every recording and transcript is treated as confidential. Access is limited to the people assigned to the file, each bound by confidentiality.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We disclose it only to the service providers who help us deliver the work, all bound by written confidentiality and data-protection undertakings, and where the law, a court order or a regulator requires it — in which case we tell you, so far as we are permitted.

If a file is sealed, held in camera or subject to a protective order, say so when you send it and we will apply the restriction.

L.04

Where it is held, and how it is protected

Files and correspondence are held in encrypted storage, and every transfer is encrypted. Access is restricted to the people assigned to the file.

Because we serve clients in Quebec, elsewhere in Canada and in the United States, personal information may be communicated outside Quebec. Before it is, we assess the protection it will receive and rely on contractual safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission where the GDPR applies.

If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we notify the people concerned and the Commission d’accès à l’information, or the competent authority, within the time the law requires.

L.05

How long we keep it

Recordings are deleted within 30 days of delivery. Transcripts and attestations are kept for two years. Billing records are kept for seven years, as tax law requires. The full schedule is in our Data Retention Policy.

You may ask at any time for your materials to be permanently deleted.

L.06

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to a use, receive it in a portable format, or withdraw a consent you have given.

Write to brian.fno@proton.me or secure@lexoral.org and we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to the authority where you live.

L.07

This website

The site sets no cookies and carries no advertising or tracking pixels. We count page views with a tool that records no IP address and no identifier, so the totals cannot identify a visitor.

Messages sent through the form reach our mailbox. The form does not accept recordings.

L.08

Professionals we write to

We write to lawyers, official stenographers and other legal professionals about their work, at an address they or their firm have published. We keep the name, the firm, the address and our correspondence, and delete it three years after our last email. We never sell or share it.

Every message says how to stop: reply and say so. The address comes off our list the same day and stays only on a suppression list, so that we never write to it again.

L.09

Changes and contact

We update this policy when our practices or the law change. Material changes are emailed to active clients, and the date at the top of this page is the latest revision.
