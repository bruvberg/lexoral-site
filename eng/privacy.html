Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Lexoral is committed to protecting the confidentiality, privacy, and security of client information and legal materials handled through our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information in connection with our legal transcription services:

  • Client contact and billing information
  • Audio or video recordings submitted for transcription
  • Transcripts, proceeding details, and case-related materials
  • Communications sent through intake forms, email, or support channels

2. Use of Information

Information provided to Lexoral is used solely for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Delivering legal transcription and related services
  • Client communication and support
  • Workflow management and quality assurance
  • Secure delivery of completed materials
  • Compliance with legal or professional obligations

3. Confidentiality & Security

All recordings, transcripts, and client documents are handled through restricted-access workflows and confidential processing procedures. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect client information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

4. AI & Model Training

Client recordings, transcripts, and related materials are never used to train public AI models and are never shared with unauthorized third parties for machine learning purposes.

5. Data Retention

Files and related materials may be retained temporarily for operational, quality assurance, support, or legal workflow purposes unless permanent deletion is requested earlier by the client.

For more detailed information regarding file retention periods and deletion practices, please refer to our Retention Policy.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Clients may request the permanent deletion of recordings, transcripts, or related materials at any time. Requests will be processed promptly, subject only to overriding legal obligations.
