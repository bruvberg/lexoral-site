The firm.

At a glance

[LX.8]

Legal transcription and litigation support, in French and English, in Quebec, across Canada and in the United States.

L.01

Who we are

Lexoral is a legal transcription and litigation support firm, founded and led by Brian Fino and Raymond Carby-Samuels. We put legal proceedings in writing, in French, in English or in both: for law firms, tribunals, legal aid plans and individuals.

Our mailing address is in Kanata, Ontario. We work anywhere in Quebec, and on files from the rest of Canada and the United States: recordings travel by encrypted transfer, and examinations are taken virtually or in person, depending on the file.

L.02

What we do

  • Examinations & depositions — taken by an official stenographer, anywhere in Quebec
  • Hearing transcription — hearings, arbitrations, administrative tribunals, mediation
  • Certification & attestation — certified transcripts for a filing or an appeal, accuracy declarations, affidavits
  • Certified translation — by a certified translator, a member of the OTTIAQ
  • Recorded evidence — 911, police interviews, intercepts, private recordings
  • Cross-border matters — Canada and the United States

Each service in detail, and what you receive: Services.

L.03

Our partners

Some acts are reserved. In Quebec, a transcript filed before a court is certified by an official stenographer, and a certified translation by a certified translator. We work with official stenographers on the Barreau du Québec’s roll of stenographers and with certified translators who are members of the OTTIAQ: Lexoral prepares and coordinates, the partner certifies, and you deal with one point of contact.

L.04

What we do not do

Lexoral is not a law firm: we give no legal advice and represent no one before a court. We are neither an official stenographer nor a certified translator: when an act is reserved to them, they are the ones who sign it. And no automated tool delivers a transcript in our place: a person listens to the entire recording, corrects the text and signs.

L.05

The record, protected

Recordings arrive by encrypted transfer and are logged from receipt to delivery. Only the people working on the file have access to it, partners included, and all of them are bound by confidentiality. Recordings are deleted within 30 days of delivery unless you ask otherwise, under our retention policy and Loi 25 (privacy policy).
