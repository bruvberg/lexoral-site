How a transcript is made

Verbatim.

[adv.] Word for word: from the recording, to the stroke, to the line. Every word, accounted for.

01The recording

The hearing, the examination, the call — sent to us by encrypted transfer.

02The stenotype

The court reporter’s shorthand: one stroke per word, unreadable to anyone else.

03The transcript

Every word in its place, line-numbered, in the language spoken. Attested — or certified.

Certified.

[adj.] Signed by an official stenographer — the only one who may, before a Quebec court. Lexoral works with one.

Attested

Lexoral’s signed declaration of accuracy — for discovery, hearing and appeal preparation, and counsel’s file.

Certified

For filing before a Quebec court: an official stenographer, accredited in French and English, holds the mandate and certifies; Lexoral prepares the bilingual transcript.
