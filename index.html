Legal transcription
in French, in English,
ou les deux.

(We’re the record.)

Lexoral produces the written record of legal proceedings: transcripts of hearings and recordings, examinations taken by an official stenographer, certified translations. In French, in English, or both. For a law firm, a tribunal, or one person with one recording, in Quebec, across Canada and in the United States. A fixed quote before we start, a delivery date in writing.

  • Law firms, legal departments, tribunals, legal aid: your file and purchase-order numbers on every invoice, your supplier forms completed, each recording received by encrypted transfer and logged from receipt to delivery.
  • Individuals: you do not need a lawyer to write to us. Tell us what you have and who the document is for; you receive a fixed quote before you commit. Lexoral is not a law firm and gives no legal advice.

LEXORAL · SPECIMEN

We arrange the examination with an official stenographer — virtually anywhere in Quebec, or in person depending on the file — and deliver the certified transcript in the deposition format. Depositions of Canadian witnesses for U.S. matters, too.

[Learn more]

Hearings, arbitrations, administrative tribunals, mediation and inquests, transcribed from the recording: speakers attributed by role, overlapping speech marked as such, each passage in the language it was spoken.

[Learn more]

Two levels. Attested: signed by Lexoral, for work on the file and wherever a stenographer’s certificate is not required. Certified: signed by an official stenographer, for a filing or an appeal before a Quebec court. An affidavit on request.

[Learn more]

Transcripts, exhibits and documents translated French to English or English to French by a certified translator, a member of the OTTIAQ, who signs and seals the translation. The translator’s affidavit on request.

[Learn more]

Emergency calls, police interviews, body-worn recordings, intercepts and private recordings, a voicemail or a recorded call included. Difficult audio: the quote follows a first listen. The translation in a parallel column and a descriptive summary on request.

[Learn more]

A Canadian witness examined for a U.S. matter, a U.S. party filing in Quebec, parallel proceedings, letters rogatory. One sitting, one record — formatted for the forum it is going to.
