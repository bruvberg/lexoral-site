Lexoral transcribes hearings, examinations and recorded evidence for counsel, tribunals, institutions and individuals in Canada and the United States — word for word, in the language each passage was spoken, in five business days or in 48 hours.

  • For law firms, legal aid plans, tribunals, regulators and individuals — supplier forms completed, your purchase order on every invoice, every file logged from receipt to delivery.
  • Attested by Lexoral, or certified by an official stenographer for filing before a Quebec court — either way, we handle it.
