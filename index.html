troisCertification & Attestation

Attested: signed by Lexoral.  Certified: signed by an official stenographer, for a Quebec court.  An affidavit on request.

Two levels, depending on the use. Attested: the transcript carries Lexoral’s signed accuracy declaration; it is the working version, and the one used wherever a stenographer’s certificate is not required. Certified: for a filing or an appeal before a Quebec court, an official stenographer on the Barreau du Québec’s roll of stenographers certifies it; Lexoral handles the request. The court office or your lawyer will tell you which one you need.

You receive the certified transcript with its certificate, or the attested transcript; an affidavit on request.

Transcripts, exhibits and documents translated and certified by a certified translator, a member of the Ordre des traducteurs, terminologues et interprètes agréés du Québec (OTTIAQ). Only one document to translate? That is a service in its own right.
