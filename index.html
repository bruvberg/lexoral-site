The standard

A legal transcript is exact, or it is not a transcript.

We hold every file to that standard: the deposition, the arbitration, the board meeting, the research interview.

What we deliver

Two forms of transcript.
One standard.

01

Attested

Signed declaration of accuracy.

By the practitioner who transcribed it. For discovery, hearing and appeal preparation, and counsel’s file.

02

Certified

Certificate of an official stenographer.

For Quebec court filings that require it. An official stenographer accredited by the Barreau du Québec holds the mandate and certifies against the recording; Lexoral prepares the bilingual transcript in collaboration.
