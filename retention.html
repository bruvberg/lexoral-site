Section 01

Purpose

This Data Retention Policy sets out the periods for which Lexoral retains different categories of personal information and proceeding materials, and the procedures by which materials are securely deleted at the end of each retention period.

Lexoral is committed to retaining personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with applicable legal obligations, and support legitimate business operations. We do not retain data speculatively or indefinitely.

This policy is read alongside our Privacy Policy. In the event of any conflict, the Privacy Policy prevails.

Section 02

Scope

This policy applies to all personal information and proceeding materials held by Lexoral, including:

  • Audio and video recordings submitted by clients for transcription
  • Completed transcripts, interim drafts, and certification documents
  • Exhibits, glossaries, and reference materials submitted with proceedings
  • Client contact and engagement records
  • Billing, invoicing, and payment records
  • Email and written communications related to a matter
  • Technical logs and access records

Section 04

Audio Recordings

Audio and video recordings submitted for transcription represent the most sensitive category of material handled by Lexoral. They contain the verbatim spoken content of legal proceedings and may include privileged communications, personal information of deponents, and confidential business matters.

Our standard practice is to delete all submitted recordings within 30 days of transcript delivery. Clients who require earlier deletion may request this at intake or at any point during the engagement.

Lexoral does not use submitted recordings for any purpose other than producing the requested transcript. Recordings are never retained for training, benchmarking, or quality assessment purposes involving identifiable content.

Deletion of recordings is performed using secure erasure methods that render the data unrecoverable. Upon request, Lexoral will provide written confirmation of deletion.

Section 05

Transcripts & Certification Documents

Completed transcripts and associated certification documents (signed accuracy statements, delivery confirmations) are retained for a default period of 2 years from the date of delivery. This period supports:

  • Client requests for re-delivery or replacement copies
  • Verification of accuracy or formatting in the event of a dispute
  • Compliance with potential legal proceedings in which the transcript is used as evidence

Clients who do not require retention of completed transcripts may request deletion at any time following delivery. Clients who require longer retention for ongoing litigation purposes may request extended retention in writing.

Section 06

Client Records

Client contact information, engagement history, and billing records are retained for the periods specified in the schedule above. Billing records are subject to a mandatory 7-year retention period under applicable Canadian and French tax legislation, and this period cannot be shortened on request.

Matter correspondence (emails, WhatsApp messages, and written communications relating to a specific proceeding) is retained for 2 years post-delivery and then permanently deleted unless a legal hold applies.

Section 07

Deletion Procedures

At the end of each applicable retention period, Lexoral permanently and securely deletes the relevant data. Our deletion procedures include:

  • Overwrite-based secure erasure for digital files stored on Lexoral-controlled systems
  • Verified deletion requests to all third-party service providers holding a copy of the data
  • Deletion confirmation logged internally with date and method
  • Written deletion confirmation provided to clients upon request

Lexoral conducts a quarterly review of all stored materials against this retention schedule and initiates deletion of materials that have exceeded their retention period.

Section 08

Client-Requested Deletion

Clients may request permanent deletion of any or all materials held by Lexoral relating to their matter at any time, subject to the following:

  • Deletion requests should be submitted to secure@lexoral.org in writing, identifying the specific matter and materials to be deleted
  • Lexoral will action deletion requests within 7 business days
  • Written confirmation of deletion will be provided upon completion
  • Billing records subject to mandatory tax retention cannot be deleted before the 7-year period expires
  • Where a legal hold is in place, deletion may be deferred until the hold is lifted
Section 09

Legal Holds

Where Lexoral becomes aware — through client notification or its own legal counsel — that materials may be relevant to ongoing or anticipated litigation, regulatory investigation, or court order, a legal hold may be placed on those materials, suspending the normal deletion schedule.

Materials subject to a legal hold will be retained until the hold is formally lifted by the instructing party or Lexoral's legal counsel. Lexoral will notify clients of any legal hold placed on their materials and its basis to the extent permitted by law.

Section 10

Review & Accountability

This Data Retention Policy is reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in Lexoral's operations, applicable law, or regulatory guidance. The person responsible for the protection of personal information at Lexoral is accountable for the implementation and enforcement of this policy.

Clients and individuals with concerns about Lexoral's data retention practices may contact us directly or file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI) or the CNIL (France).
