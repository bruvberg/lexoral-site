[What we do]

To scale.

From a single examination to a tribunal’s full docket — every transcript built to the forum’s own template. The full specification is at the bottom of this page, and the specimen transcript is downloadable.

01Depositions & examinationsDiscovery, depositions, examinations under oath.

Examinations for discovery, depositions, cross-examinations on affidavit, examinations under oath (EUO), out-of-court examinations and recorded statements. Delivered in the deposition format: Q./A. notation, continuous line numbering, speakers identified, objections noted — each passage in the language it was spoken.

02Hearings & arbitrationsLabour, commercial and institutional arbitration; mediation.

Labour and grievance arbitration, commercial arbitration, institutional panels (ICC, AAA, ICDR, ADRIC, CIAC), mediation and settlement conferences, multi-party hearings. Speakers attributed by role; overlapping speech marked as such, never smoothed into clean sentences.

03Tribunals & regulatory hearingsAdministrative tribunals, boards, discipline, immigration.

Administrative tribunals, labour and employment boards, human rights tribunals, professional discipline councils, immigration and refugee hearings, coroner’s inquests and commissions of inquiry. Inaudible passages timestamped; uncertain names marked [phonetic].

04Recorded evidenceCalls, interviews, 911, body-worn and custodial recordings.

Emergency-services calls, custodial and police-station interviews, body-worn recordings, intercepts and voicemail — difficult audio, quoted after we have heard it. Foreign-language passages transcribed as spoken, with the translation in a parallel column on request.

Recorded evidence→
05Certified transcriptsFor filing before a Quebec court.

Certified by an official stenographer accredited in French and English. Lexoral handles the request from start to finish — the recording, the bilingual transcript, the certification — with one point of contact.

Certified transcripts→
06Cross-border mattersCanada and the United States: one record, two forums.

A Canadian witness examined for a U.S. matter, a U.S. party filing in Quebec, parallel proceedings, letters rogatory. One sitting, one record — formatted for the forum it is going to.
